A Deep Dive into the Practice of Performance Analysis

An independent study at the University of Utah, 2023, with Connor Scully-Allison and Dr. Kate Isaacs.

About the study

We explored how developers find performance problems, choose analysis tools, and learn to use them. Through focus group conversations and qualitative analysis, we looked for common patterns in the way people approach performance work.

What we found

There is no single workflow or tool that fits every performance problem. Developers adapt their approach to the system they are studying, while large profiler outputs, limited tool access, and uneven documentation can make the work harder. The study points toward clearer guidance and more accessible tools.
